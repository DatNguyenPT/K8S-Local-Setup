LEASE RBAC SETUP GUIDE
=======================

This guide walks you through setting up the necessary RBAC permissions for a Kubernetes CronJob or Pod to use Leases for coordination (e.g., ensuring only one backup job runs at a time).

---

STEP 1: APPLY THE SERVICE ACCOUNT
----------------------------------
This ServiceAccount will be assigned to your CronJob (e.g., `db-backup`) to give it permission to interact with Lease objects.

Apply it with:

    kubectl apply -f Lease/lease-sa.yaml

---

STEP 2: DEFINE THE ROLE
------------------------
The Role grants fine-grained access to Leases within a specific namespace (e.g., `dbs`).

Apply it with:

    kubectl apply -f Lease/role.yaml

---

STEP 3: BIND THE ROLE TO THE SERVICE ACCOUNT
---------------------------------------------
The RoleBinding connects the above Role with the ServiceAccount, enabling lease operations.

Apply it with:

    kubectl apply -f Lease/role-binding.yaml

---

VERIFY SETUP
-------------
Check if the RoleBinding is working:

    kubectl get rolebinding -n dbs
    kubectl describe rolebinding lease-role-binding -n dbs

Check that the ServiceAccount is attached to your CronJob:

```yaml
spec:
  serviceAccountName: lease-sa
